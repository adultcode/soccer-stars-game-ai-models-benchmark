### PHYSICS & MATHEMATICS ENGINE REQUIREMENTS
Implement realistic 2D rigid-body vector physics for circles:
1. Friction / Deceleration: Apply exponential friction on every frame to all moving bodies (`velocity *= friction`, where friction is ~0.985 for caps and ~0.990 for the ball). If absolute velocity drops below 0.05px/frame, snap velocity to zero.
2. Wall / Pitch Boundary Collisions:
   - Bouncing off top/bottom pitch walls and goalpost edges must invert the velocity along the collision normal with a bounce coefficient (~0.85).
3. 2D Circle-to-Circle Elastic Collisions:
   - Calculate distance between center points $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
   - Overlap Resolution: If $d < r_1 + r_2$, separate the circles along the normal vector immediately to avoid overlap sticking/glitching.
   - Momentum Transfer: Calculate impulse along the collision normal using the conservation of momentum and mass ratios:
     $$v_1' = v_1 - \frac{2 m_2}{m_1 + m_2} \frac{\langle v_1 - v_2, x_1 - x_2 \rangle}{\Vert{}x_1 - x_2\Vert{}^2} (x_1 - x_2)$$
4. Motion Resolution State:
   - Maintain a global state `isMoving`. It is `true` as long as ANY cap or the ball has non-zero velocity.
   - No inputs or AI triggers can occur while `isMoving === true`.

### TURN FLOW & GAME RULES
1. Gameplay Flow:
   - Turn sequence: Player Turn -> Waiting for physics to stop -> AI Turn -> Waiting for physics to stop -> Player Turn.

2. Drag & Shoot Slingshot Mechanics (Player Turn):

   - Release: Releasing mouse/touch applies vector velocity $v = (\text{CapPos} - \text{MousePos}) \times \text{PowerFactor}$ to the selected cap and switches team turn state.
3. Goal Detection & Reset:
   - When the ball completely crosses the goal line into either net, trigger a "GOAL!" notification overlay.
   - Increment the respective score.
   - After a 1.5-second goal pause, reset all 6 caps and the ball back to their starting kick-off positions.
4. Game Over & Reset Button:
   - Top Bar UI: Displays Score ("Player: X | AI: Y"), Turn status indicator ("Your Turn" / "AI Thinking..."), and a prominent "Reset Game" button.
   - When a team reaches 2 goals, display a modal dialog: "VICTORY!" or "GAME OVER!", disabling further shots until "Reset Game" is clicked.

### AI DECISION ENGINE (Computer Turn)
1. Trigger: When it is the AI's turn AND `isMoving === false`, wait 1000ms (to feel natural).
2. Shot Calculation Heuristic:
   - Loop through all 3 AI blue caps.
   - Calculate the angle/vector from each AI cap to the ball, and from the ball to the player's net (Left Goal target).
   - Find the best cap that has a clear direct angle to hit the ball toward the goal.
   - Calculate shot power relative to distance.
3. Execution: Apply the calculated velocity vector to the chosen AI cap and trigger the physics phase.
